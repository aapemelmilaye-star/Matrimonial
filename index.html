Our Work

Aape Mel Milaye helps families share matrimonial profiles within the Sindhi, Punjabi and Sikh community. The service is intended to support sincere families, reduce commercial pressure, and make introductions easier through organized profile collection and member-only access.

How It Works

  • Families submit candidate details, photograph and login-request details through one Google Form.
  • After admin approval, the profile is published and login details are emailed automatically.
  • Members request login access and are approved by the admin.
  • Approved boys see girls' profiles, and approved girls see boys' profiles.
